Post Scheduling (Drafts -> Scheduled -> Posted)                     *bsky-sched*
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design Principles:
- Ship quickly with testable architecture
- Swap schedulers later without refactoring
- Share posting logic across all modes
- Mobile OSes don't guarantee exact timing

User Stories:
- Schedule draft for future time
- View/cancel/reschedule upcoming posts
- See failures with clear reasons and retry
- Choose between notification-based or background auto-post

Non-Goals: Server-side scheduling (see *bsky-sched-backend*), enterprise job
orchestration, exact-time guarantees
