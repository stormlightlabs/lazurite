### Alt Text Enforcement (sort of)

WitchSky requires alt text for images and videos. I want to have that be an option/setting
specific to this app. If alt text isn't enforced by a user, we'll provide as much context
as possible and an option to turn on the enforcement setting.

- [Guidelines](https://www.wcag.com/blog/good-alt-text-bad-alt-text-making-your-content-perceivable/)
